Biography

I am a researcher in wireless communications, signal processing, and randomized numerical linear and tensor algebra. I defended my Ph.D. thesis, Scalable Signal Processing Algorithms for Large-Scale and Intelligent Wireless Networks, at the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi on 26 October 2026. The formal degree award is pending. My doctoral advisors are Dr. Adarsh Patel (IIT Mandi) and Dr. Rameshwar Pratap (IIT Hyderabad).

My doctoral research develops scalable algorithms for large-scale wireless systems using random projections, sampling, structured least-squares optimization, and tensor computations. The work addresses detection in massive/XL-MIMO networks, channel estimation and semi-blind detection in IRS-assisted networks, and randomized CP and Tucker tensor decomposition.

I am particularly interested in exploiting multilinear and tensor structure for estimation and detection in next-generation wireless systems, alongside scalable randomized methods for large-scale tensor computations.
